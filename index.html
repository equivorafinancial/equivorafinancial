Pricing Questions

What if my transaction volume changes?

We monitor your volume monthly. If you consistently exceed your plan limit, we'll discuss upgrading to the next tier with 30 days notice. Temporary spikes (like seasonal businesses) don't trigger automatic upgrades.

Are there setup fees?

No setup fees for monthly bookkeeping. If you need extensive cleanup or migration, that's quoted separately as a one-time project. Many clients start with cleanup, then move to monthly service.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, with 30 days written notice. We'll provide a clean handoff with all your files organized and ready for your next bookkeeper or to bring in-house. No termination fees.

How do you bill?

Monthly billing in advance, typically on the 1st of each month. We accept ACH bank transfers, credit cards, and checks. First month is prorated based on start date.
